    Contour lab

    A workflow is a list of steps. Each step is a module: Image modules change the grey-level image, Mask modules mark the pattern's pixels, Contours modules make contours and choose, move or smooth them. The list on the right runs from top to bottom on the image in front, and says beside every step what it found and how long it took. Untick a step to see the result without it.

    Examples holds workflows to start from. Show: selected step shows what the workflow had made after the step you click in the list. Pin keeps the contours on screen as a second layer while you change the workflow, to compare the two.

    Tabs hold one image each. With all tabs, every tab runs the same workflow: switch tabs to see it on the other images. With this tab, the tab in front keeps a workflow of its own: duplicate a tab and give the copy another workflow to compare the two on one image.

    TIFF pages: the page box and its arrows go through the pages; Stack joins pages into one strip (reverse and flip for stripes scanned upwards). A workflow runs on the region: the whole image, or on large strips the part you choose with Shift + drag or Current view.
