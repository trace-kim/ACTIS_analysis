    How the lab works

    Left: what you choose. A method is a short list of steps that run from top to bottom on the image in front. Choose a method offers ready ones. Click a step to see and change its settings; its switch turns it off without removing it.

    Right: what you get. How many contours, and the numbers of one measure at a time: its typical value, its spread, its histogram. The same measure colours the contours, so the histogram is also the key to the colours. Click a contour in the image for its own numbers.

    Tabs hold one image each and run the same method, so switching tabs compares images. To compare two methods on one image, duplicate its tab (More) and give the copy another method, or keep the contours of one as a reference and choose another.

    TIFF pages. The bar under the image goes through the pages. Stack joins pages into one long strip.
